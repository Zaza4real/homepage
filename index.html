How it works

From one upload to fully localized versions — fast, consistent, and on-brand.

  1. 1
    Upload your video

    Drop in MP4 or a link. YouDub extracts audio + transcription.

  2. 2
    Translate + preserve your voice

    We translate into target languages and keep the output sounding like you.

  3. 3
    Lip-sync to the new language

    Mouth movement is aligned to the translated speech for a natural result.

  4. 4
    Export & publish

    Download localized videos and ship globally in minutes.
