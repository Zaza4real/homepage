Notes

Short thoughts on building: clarity, speed, and quality.

Make it feel fast

Don’t overload the page. Use space, strong type, and one accent. Motion should be purposeful.

Keep secrets server-side

AI tokens never go into the browser. Use your backend service to call Replicate securely.

Ship v1, then polish

Launch something real, then iterate. A clean baseline beats a “perfect someday”.
